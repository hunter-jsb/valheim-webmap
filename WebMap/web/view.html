Getting about

  • Drag to look around; scroll or pinch to zoom the eye.
  • W A S D or the arrows walk, Shift runs, Q E turn.
  • Click the ground to walk there.
  • Overview circles the spot from above: drag to pan, right-drag or two fingers to turn.
  • Only ground somebody has walked is drawn; the rest stays dark, as on the map.
